SMS Relay Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Developer: DevStudio KS
Privacy contact: kyusung.dev@gmail.com

Information processed and purposes

SMS Relay forwards SMS or app notifications to a phone number based on the conditions you configure and the data use you consent to.

When SMS forwarding is on, the app reads the sender number and body of new incoming SMS, checks your keyword and forwards matching content. When notification forwarding is on, it processes the selected app’s notification title, body, sender information, package name and notification identifiers to compose SMS and prevent duplicates. Message and notification bodies are not stored in the app’s history.

After you consent to the notification feature, installed app names and package names are accessed to display an app selection list. Your destination number, keyword, selected app, feature switches, and each feature’s consent version and time are stored in the app’s private storage.

The app keeps the latest 50 forwarding records containing the source, destination number, time, sending result, SMS part count and result code. Hashes of sending content and destination, part counts and times are used for duplicate suppression and sending limits over a 10-minute window. Expired entries are excluded when the next sending request is processed.

Android notification access may expose notifications from multiple apps. After consent, SMS Relay processes only your selected app. The receive-SMS permission is used after consent to check keywords in new SMS. The app does not request contacts or historical SMS storage access.

Transmission and third parties

Once you turn a feature on, matching content is sent to your configured destination even when the app is closed. SMS may contain personal information such as the original content, sender number or notification sender.

Sending uses your mobile carrier’s SMS service and the recipient at the configured number receives the content. Carrier processing and retention follow the carrier’s policies. SMS is not end-to-end encrypted. The app cannot delete or recall messages on a receiving device or information held by carriers.

The developer does not receive SMS or notification content from the app. The app includes no advertising, analytics or crash-collection SDKs and does not sell data or use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Settings, consent state, forwarding history and duplicate/rate-limit information are kept in Android’s private app storage. Android app-data backup is disabled. Content is processed in memory while forwarding and is not saved in the app’s history. Android SMS display/retention and carrier retention may differ by device and carrier.

History is limited to 50 records; older records are removed when new ones are added. You can delete forwarding history on the History tab. To delete all app data, including settings and consent state, clear the app’s data in Android App info or uninstall it. This does not delete messages on receiving devices or information retained by carriers.

Your choices

SMS and notification forwarding require separate consent. Before consent, the app does not access the corresponding data or request its permissions. Consent alone does not enable automatic forwarding; you activate each feature using its switch.

You can withdraw each feature’s consent under Data use consent in Settings. Withdrawal stops that forwarding feature. Turning a feature’s switch off stops forwarding new events. You can also revoke SMS permissions or notification access in Android settings. Sending already submitted to the carrier cannot be cancelled.

The app does not automatically identify and exclude all verification codes, passwords or other sensitive content matching your keyword or selected app. Choose your destination number and the content you share carefully.

Changes and contact

This policy is updated if functionality or data handling changes. For privacy enquiries, contact kyusung.dev@gmail.com.
